Open search with the magnifier at the top right of any screen, or press **⌘K** on a Mac and
**Ctrl+K** elsewhere.

## Before you type

- **Recent**: the last six records you opened from search, kept on this browser. Hover one and
  choose **×**, or press **Delete** on it, to remove it; **Clear all** empties the list.
- **Create**: **New contact**, **New company** (while Companies is on), **New campaign**,
  **New automation**, and **New task** (while Tasks is on), each only if you may add one. Each
  opens the form for it.
- **Pages**: a few of the CRM's screens. Type to find any screen by name.

## Searching

Type two letters or more. Search looks through:

| Kind | Matches |
|---|---|
| Contacts | First name, last name, email address or phone number |
| Companies | Name or domain |
| Deals | Title, company name or the primary contact's name |
| Campaigns | Title or subject |
| Automations and Sequences | Title |
| Templates | Title or category |
| Tags and Lists | Name; opening one shows the contacts who have it |
| Notes | The title and text of notes, calls, meetings and emails you logged |
| Tasks | Title, among the tasks you see on **Tasks**; opening one shows it there |
| Files | File name; opening one shows it on **Files** |

Records whose name or title (for contacts, first name) starts with what you typed come first.
Notes and files come newest first, tasks in the order of the **Tasks** page. Each kind shows its
first five; the row of kinds under the field lists those that found something and how many
(**5+** means more). Choose a kind, or **Show more** beside its heading, or press **Tab** and
**Shift+Tab**, to search that kind alone and see up to twenty.
**Backspace** in an empty field goes back to **All**.

**↑** and **↓** move, **Enter** opens, **Esc** closes.

You only see what you may open: a kind your role cannot view is not searched, notes and files
on a record show only when you can see that record, and a deal in a pipeline you are not on does
not appear. Deals, Tasks and Files are searched only while their add-on is on.

## When it goes wrong

- **No matches**: try part of a name or an email address. One letter matches only pages.
- **Search failed**: the request did not get an answer. Choose **Retry**.
