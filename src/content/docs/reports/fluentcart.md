Open **Reports → FluentCart**. It needs Marker CRM Pro and the
[FluentCart integration](/settings/integrations/#fluentcart) turned on. It is the same report as the
[WooCommerce report](/reports/woocommerce/) — totals, chart and comparison, email revenue, top selling products,
subscriptions, customer stages and customer health — read from your FluentCart orders. What differs:

- **Every buyer counts.** FluentCart keeps a customer for every order, so spend per customer and the stages include
  people who bought without an account.
- **Revenue** counts orders paid, partly paid or partly refunded, net of refunds. A fully refunded order counts for
  nothing.
- **Kinds of order** come from FluentCart's own: a subscription's first order, a renewal, or a one-time order.
- **Refunded** at the top is everything refunded, and its share of what was taken before refunds.
- **A subscription ends** on the day it is cancelled, or on its expiry once it has expired. **Active subscriptions**
  at the top shows what they bring in a month, each one's amount brought to a month by its billing interval.

A stage opens as a segment named *FluentCart: …*. Its cut-offs are set on the card or under **Customer stages** on the
FluentCart **Manage** page.

FluentCart's own **Reports** screen covers the shop itself — payment methods, refunds, coupons and more; this one is
about your customers and what your emails earned.
